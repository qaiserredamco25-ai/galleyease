7 Smart Ways to Use the Space Above Kitchen Cabinets

The space above kitchen cabinets is often overlooked, especially in small homes and apartments. While it may seem too high or awkward to use, this area can provide extra room for selected kitchen items or simple decorative touches.

The key is choosing items that are safe to store, easy to access, and suitable for the available height. You do not need to fill every empty space. A few thoughtful arrangements can make the kitchen feel more organized without adding clutter to your counters or cabinets.

7 Practical Ideas for the Space Above Kitchen Cabinets

1. Store Infrequently Used Serving Items

Large serving platters, decorative bowls, and special-occasion dishes may not need to stay within everyday reach. If the area above your cabinets is suitable, it can provide a place for some of these occasional-use items.

Use sturdy containers or baskets to keep similar items together. Avoid storing heavy or fragile dishes high above the floor, particularly where they could fall or be difficult to lift safely.

2. Use Matching Storage Baskets

Matching baskets can make the space above cabinets look more organized. They can hold lightweight, non-perishable kitchen supplies that are used occasionally.

Choose baskets with handles so they are easier to move when needed. Measure the available height and depth first, and avoid placing containers where they extend beyond the cabinet edge.

3. Display a Small Collection of Decorative Pieces

If you prefer a decorative approach, the space above your cabinets can display a small collection of suitable kitchen accessories. A few coordinated pieces can add character without taking up valuable working space.

Keep the arrangement simple and leave enough room to clean around the objects. Avoid fragile decorations in locations where vibration or accidental contact could make them fall.

4. Arrange Lightweight Seasonal Kitchen Items

Some kitchen items are only needed during particular seasons or celebrations. Lightweight seasonal decorations, serving accessories, or occasional-use table items may be stored above cabinets if the space is suitable.

Place them in labelled containers to make them easier to identify later. Make sure stored items are clean and dry before putting them away.

5. Add a Suitable Shelf for Extra Storage

Where the cabinet design and available clearance allow it, a properly installed shelf above the cabinets may create an additional storage level.

Before considering this option, check the wall construction, cabinet arrangement, shelf specifications, and weight limits. Use appropriate installation hardware and seek professional help if necessary. Renters should obtain permission before making permanent changes.

6. Use Closed Containers to Reduce Dust

The space above kitchen cabinets can collect dust and cooking residue, particularly in kitchens without a full-height cabinet design. Closed containers can help protect suitable stored items.

Choose containers with secure lids and surfaces that are easy to wipe clean. Avoid storing food that requires controlled temperature or humidity in an area that does not provide those conditions.

7. Keep the Space Partly Empty

One of the simplest ways to improve the appearance of this area is to resist the temptation to fill it completely. Leaving some open space can make the kitchen look less crowded.

Select only a few useful or decorative items. An uncluttered arrangement is easier to clean and reduces the risk of objects being pushed too close to the edge.

What Should You Avoid Storing Above Kitchen Cabinets?

Not everything belongs in this elevated space. Consider the following precautions before using it for storage:

  • Avoid heavy appliances, glass containers, and bulky cookware that may be difficult to lift safely.
  • Do not store food that needs refrigeration or controlled storage conditions.
  • Keep items away from heat sources, particularly above cooking appliances.
  • Avoid placing objects near ceiling fixtures, vents, or other areas where they may obstruct airflow or access.
  • Do not overcrowd the area or place objects where they can fall onto people or work surfaces.
  • Clean the area regularly to prevent dust and grease buildup.

How to Decide Whether This Space Is Suitable for Storage

Before using the space above your cabinets, check its height, depth, accessibility, and condition. Consider whether you can safely reach the stored items without standing on an unstable chair or stretching over a hot cooking area.

If access is difficult, it may be better to leave the space empty or use it only for suitable decorative items. Storage should make your kitchen more practical, not introduce a safety problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it a good idea to store things above kitchen cabinets?

It can be useful for suitable lightweight and occasional-use items, provided they can be stored securely and accessed safely. The area should also be cleaned regularly.

How can I make the space above kitchen cabinets look organized?

Use a small number of coordinated baskets or decorative items. Avoid overcrowding, and keep the arrangement balanced and easy to maintain.

Do items above kitchen cabinets collect dust?

Yes. This area can collect dust and cooking residue. Closed containers and regular cleaning can help keep stored items cleaner.

Can I store kitchen appliances above cabinets?

Generally, heavy or bulky appliances are not a good choice because they can be difficult and unsafe to lift down. Store them in an accessible cabinet or suitable lower storage area instead.

How often should I clean above kitchen cabinets?

The cleaning frequency depends on how much cooking residue and dust accumulate in your kitchen. Check the area regularly and clean it whenever buildup becomes noticeable.

Final Tip: The space above kitchen cabinets can be useful, but it does not need to become another crowded storage area. Choose suitable items, prioritize safe access, and keep the arrangement simple.
